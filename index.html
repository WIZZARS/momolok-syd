The Mo Molok Heritage

A Taste of Home,
Rooted in Dharan

Mo Molok was born out of nostalgia for the bustling night markets of Dharan, Eastern Nepal — where foggy hill breezes meet the comforting aroma of steaming momo baskets and sizzled spices.

Every dumpling at Mo Molok is pleated by hand using fresh ingredients and traditional Himalayan spice blends. From slow-simmered sesame Jhol broths to iconic Dharane Egg Rolls, we bring authentic hill gastronomy to Ashfield with modern elegance.

Our Roots & Culture

The City That Inspired Us

Dharan — where mountain morning mist meets vibrant street markets, warm hill culture, and legendary gastronomy.

Himalayan Foothills

Dharan, Nepal

Elevation: 349m · Sunsari District

01

The Culinary Crossroads

Nestled at the base of the Eastern hills, Dharan connects mountain spice traditions with fresh Terai ingredients, giving birth to Nepal's most revered street food culture.

02

Bhanu Chowk & Bijayapur

From the iconic Clock Tower of Bhanu Chowk to the hilltop temples of Bijayapur, food is Dharan's living language of warmth, laughter, and community gathering.

03

Spirit of the Prayer Flags

Just like the colorful lungta prayer flags floating over Dharan’s ridges, Mo Molok carries a message of peace, good food, and authentic hospitality to Sydney.
