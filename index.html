Our Story

A Taste of Home,
Far From the Hills

Mo Molok began with a craving — the kind that only real street food can satisfy. Rooted in the food culture of Dharan, Nepal, we bring the flavours of mountain markets and late-night family kitchens to Ashfield, Sydney.

Every momo is folded by hand. Every broth is slow-simmered. Every plate is built to feel like welcome — warm, honest, and unforgettable.

Our Roots

The City That Made Us

Dharan — where fog meets mountains, and every street corner smells of spice and steam.

Dharan, Nepal — Evening panorama
📍

Gateway to the Hills

Dharan sits at the foothills of the Himalayan range, where hill culture and plains culture collide beautifully — creating a food scene unlike anywhere else in Nepal.

🥟

Momo Capital

Dharan is legendary for its street-food culture. Momos, egg rolls, and thukpa are not merely meals here — they are the city's language of welcome and warmth.

🧭

Bhanu Chowk & Bijayapur

The iconic Bijayapur Clock Tower at sunset, the lively Bhanu Chowk bazaar — these are the living postcards we carry in our hearts and bring to every plate we serve.

🎏

Prayer Flags & Good Vibes

Like the lungta prayer flags that flutter above Dharan's ridges, Mo Molok carries the spirit of its home city — spreading colour, warmth, and good energy wherever it goes.
